# Privacy

Bubbles Word Writer is designed for private, local-first writing. It does not require an account or cloud storage.

## What Is Not Collected

Bubbles Word Writer does not include publisher-operated:

- Telemetry or analytics
- Advertising or behavioral tracking
- Document-content collection
- Automatic cloud uploads
- Sale, rental, or monetization of user data

The publisher does not receive your documents, settings, recovery files, templates, clipboard history, extension information, or general usage activity.

## Where Your Data Stays

Documents remain wherever you choose to save them. Application settings, recovery information, local version history, templates, clipboard history, and extension information remain on your device by default.

The uninstaller lets you choose whether to keep or remove application settings, recovery information, and extension/profile data. Documents saved in your own folders are not removed.

## Actions You Control

Bubbles Word Writer accesses a network only when you deliberately use a feature that needs one, such as:

- Checking for application updates
- Opening an external website or email link
- Saving a document in a cloud-synchronized folder
- Connecting to a collaboration service you selected
- Installing content from an external extension source

These actions may share information with the service you selected. Third-party services have their own privacy terms.

## Optional Local Features

- **Local Writing Assistant:** selected text is processed by the Ollama installation on your computer.
- **Image Text Recognition:** selected images are processed by Windows on your computer.
- **Dictation:** speech recognition uses Windows and does not create or upload an audio recording through Bubbles Word Writer.
- **Text-to-Speech:** selected text is read using voices available on your computer.

## Collaboration

Live Collaboration is off until you choose to connect. While connected, the active document, comments, cursor position, display name, and cursor color are shared with the collaboration service and participants in that room.

The publisher does not operate the collaboration service or receive a copy of the session. The service operator is responsible for its access controls, storage, logs, retention, and privacy practices.

## Your Choices

You can use Bubbles Word Writer entirely for local documents without enabling collaboration, local AI, external extensions, or cloud-synchronized storage.

You can review and change privacy-related choices in **File > Settings** and remove optional application data during uninstall.
